Sunpatch Valley - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Applies to the Sunpatch Valley game for Android.

In short: Sunpatch Valley collects no data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics, and it does not even have permission to use the internet. Your farm is saved only on your device.

What the game stores

  • Your save game (farm, items, level, progress) and your settings (volume, text size, vibration) are stored only in the game's private storage on your device.
  • Nothing from your save or settings is sent to the developer. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this information is deleted from your device.
  • If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the game's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by the developer.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking and no account system run by the developer. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Ads and purchases

There are no advertisements and no in-app purchases. Nothing in the game can be bought with real money.

Network use

The game plays fully offline. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over the network. The only permission it asks for is vibration (for the small taps you can switch off under Settings > Vibration).

Notifications

The game does not send notifications.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes any of this (for example by adding optional online features), this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date, and the store listing's data safety section will say so.
